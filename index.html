About

Profile

I am originally from Santiago, Chile. Before starting my PhD at Penn, I completed my undergraduate and master's studies at Universidad de Chile, Beauchef, where I worked with Gonzalo A. Palma on infrared divergences and loop effects in de Sitter spacetime.

Research

Interests

My research is centered on the physics of the primordial universe: multifield inflation, curvature-isocurvature mixing, cosmological collider signals, loop corrections, infrared effects, and the wavefunction of the universe.

Preprint / 2026

Pushing the Primordial Frontier: Cosmological Collider Signatures at Strong Mixing

Javier Huenupi, Claudio Munoz, Gonzalo A. Palma, Spyros Sypsas

A study of primordial non-Gaussianity in multifield inflation beyond the weak-mixing approximation. The work develops analytic tools that resum curvature-isocurvature transfer to all orders, showing how strong mixing can amplify cosmological-collider signals and reveal scaling behavior invisible to perturbative treatments.

Preprint / 2026

Pushing the Primordial Frontier: Exact Linear Solutions in Multifield Inflation

Javier Huenupi, Claudio Munoz, Gonzalo A. Palma, Spyros Sypsas

This paper builds exact analytic control over a two-field inflationary system where curvature perturbations interact with isocurvature modes. By solving the linear dynamics for arbitrary entropy mass and mixing strength, it provides a compact foundation for studying power spectra, non-Gaussianity, particle production, and loop effects in strongly mixed multifield regimes.

Journal article / 2025

A note on loop resummation in de Sitter spacetime with the wavefunction of the universe approach

Javier Huenupi, Ellie Hughes, Gonzalo A. Palma, Spyros Sypsas

A short technical note clarifying how infrared behavior appears when computing de Sitter correlators through the wavefunction of the universe. The key point is subtle: loop corrections to wavefunction coefficients may look infrared safe, but the second step converting them into correlators restores the expected infrared structure through an effective renormalization of the potential.

arXiv Journal

Journal article / 2024

Regularizing infrared divergences in de Sitter spacetime: Loops, dimensional regularization, and cutoffs

Javier Huenupi, Ellie Hughes, Gonzalo A. Palma, Spyros Sypsas

This work revisits the common expectation that loop corrections in de Sitter necessarily generate secular growth. It shows how infrared divergences can be handled systematically with counterterms, and explains why Wilsonian cutoff descriptions require time-dependent effective coefficients to keep observables independent of arbitrary infrared regulators.
